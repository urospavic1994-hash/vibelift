## 1. Full Logo Lockup (`logo-full.png`)

Use the font style of Righteous — rounded, bold, slightly retro but modern. The text should have a subtle neon pink glow around the "Vibe" portion. Include a small abstract icon to the left of the text — a stylized upward arrow or dumbbell shape made of neon pink light with a cyan (#00E5FF) accent detail. Background is deep dark navy blue (#0A0E27). The overall feel is sleek, energetic, neon nightlife but fitness-focused. No palm trees, no flamingos, no cheesy retro elements. Clean and minimal. High resolution, transparent-friendly composition. Horizontal layout.
```

## 3. App Icon / Favicon (`logo-icon.png`)

```
Design a square app icon for "VibeLift" — a fitness tracker with Vice City neon style. The icon should be a single abstract symbol — a stylized "V" or upward arrow/chevron shape that suggests both "vibe" and "lifting." The symbol is neon hot pink (#FF2D78) with a subtle pink glow, sitting on a deep dark navy (#0A0E27) background. Maybe a tiny cyan (#00E5FF) accent line or dot as a secondary detail. No text in this version — icon only. The shape should be simple enough to read at 32x32 favicon size but look great at 512x512 app icon size. Rounded square format with smooth corners. ```

## Tips for Gemini

- If the glow is too strong, ask it to "make the neon glow more subtle and refined"
- If colors are off, re-specify the hex codes in a follow-up
- Generate at the highest resolution available
- Ask for "transparent background" variant after picking the best one
- For the icon, also ask for a version "without rounded corners on a transparent background" so you can add your own corner radius in CSS/PWA manifest
