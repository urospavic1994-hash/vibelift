Privacy,
plainly

Last updated: July 18, 2026

VibeLift is a workout tracker. This page explains what data exists, where it lives, and how you get rid of it. No legal maze — if anything here is unclear, email us and we'll explain it like a human.

What we store

  • Your email address — only if you create an account. Used to send sign-in links and nothing else.
  • Your workout data — exercises, sets, reps, weights, dates, and any custom exercises or profile settings (name, kg/lb) you enter.

That's the whole list. No ad trackers, no analytics profiles, no location, no contacts, no selling or sharing your data with anyone. Ever.

Where it lives

  • On your device — the app saves everything locally first, so it works offline. This data is yours, on your hardware.
  • In the cloud (only with an account) — a mirror copy is stored with Supabase on servers in Frankfurt, Germany (European Union), so your workouts survive a lost phone and sync between devices.

Who can see it

You. Database security rules (row level security) make your rows invisible to every other user and to anyone without your login. Sign-in links go to your email only.

Deleting your data

  • Cloud data: Settings → Account → "Delete account & cloud data". This permanently erases your account and every cloud row, immediately, no email required, no questions asked.
  • Device data: clear the site data in your browser, or uninstall the app.

Cookies & tracking

No advertising cookies, no third-party trackers. The app stores a login token on your device so you stay signed in — that's it.

Changes

If this policy changes in any way that matters, the "last updated" date above changes and significant changes will be announced in the app.
