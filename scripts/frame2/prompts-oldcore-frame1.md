# Frame 1 prompts for the 5 old core moves that have no picture at all (written 19 Sep 2026).
# Format: NAME | [bodyweight] | exercise line.  Used with the icon recipe v5 BASE prompt.
# All five are bodyweight, so the BODYWEIGHT RULE sentence must be appended to every one of them.

Plank | [bodyweight] | He holds a forearm plank on a thin grey mat: both forearms flat on the mat under the shoulders, elbows bent at ninety degrees, body one straight rigid line from head to heels, hips level, toes on the mat, head looking down at the mat.
Crunches | [bodyweight] | He lies on his back on a thin grey mat, knees bent with both feet flat on the mat, both hands lightly behind his head with elbows out, head and shoulder blades curled up off the mat toward the knees, lower back still on the mat.
Leg Raise | [bodyweight] | He lies flat on his back on a thin grey mat, arms straight down at his sides with palms on the mat, both legs held straight and together and lifted up so they point at the ceiling, hips and back still on the mat.
Russian Twist | [bodyweight] | He sits on a thin grey mat leaning his torso back at about forty five degrees, knees bent with both heels lifted just off the mat, both hands clasped together in front of the chest, shoulders and arms rotated to one side.
Ab Wheel | [bodyweight] | He kneels on a thin grey mat holding an ab roller wheel with both hands, arms straight, the small wheel rolled out in front of him on the mat so his body is stretched forward in a long straight line from knees to head, hips low. The ab roller wheel is the only equipment in the picture.
