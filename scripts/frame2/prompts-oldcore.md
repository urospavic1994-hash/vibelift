# Frame 2 end positions for the 4 old core moves that got a first picture on 19 Sep 2026.
# Plank is a hold and has no second position, so it is not in this list.

Crunches | lying all the way back down flat on the mat, head and shoulder blades resting on the mat, both hands still lightly behind the head with the elbows out, knees still bent with both feet flat on the mat; the mat does not move
Leg Raise | both legs lowered together until they are straight and hovering just above the mat, feet close to the mat, arms still straight at the sides with the palms down, hips and back still flat on the mat; the mat does not move
Russian Twist | the torso and shoulders rotated to the other side, both hands still clasped together but now carried across to that side beside the hip, same seated lean-back position with the heels still lifted just off the mat; the mat does not move
Ab Wheel | kneeling upright with the ab roller wheel pulled all the way back in close to the knees, arms straight down to the wheel, hips high above the heels, the body folded up short instead of stretched out; the mat and the wheel stay the same size and the wheel is the only equipment
