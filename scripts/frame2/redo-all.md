## B. SCENE-FLIP REDOS — Gemini mirrored the bench or machine instead of moving the person

Dumbbell Squeeze Press, Incline Smith Machine Press, Decline Machine Chest Press
Add to the prompt: "the bench (or machine) stays in exactly the same place and the same direction, the head stays at
the same end, only the arms move". These scored 14-23% on frame_diff, the highest of the project, because the whole
scene moved. A high score is NOT proof of a good picture (LESSONS_LEARNED 19 Sep).
