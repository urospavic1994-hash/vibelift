## Echo rule learned
Echo risk is highest when the body barely travels: bent-over barbell rows, pull-ups from the top, hip bridges, dips, pushdowns with the bar already low, anything lying on a bench with a barbell. Put the distance in the prompt ("a full arm length lower", "thirty centimetres higher").
